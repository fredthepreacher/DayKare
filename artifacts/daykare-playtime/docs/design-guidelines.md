# DayKare Playtime design guidelines

These rules were extracted from DayKare’s current menu, lobby, settings,
journal, HUD, and accessibility treatments. They describe the source as it
exists; the dark theme is a derived accessible counterpart.

## Visual signal

- Lead with a warm paper canvas, cocoa text, and one dominant activity color.
- Use sunshine orange for primary story paths, playground teal for connection
  and secondary actions, and imagination pink for expressive customization.
- Keep controls tactile: substantial radii, visible borders, a thicker lower
  edge, and clear pressed feedback.
- Reserve Fraunces for story and destination headings. Use Plus Jakarta Sans
  for interface copy and Space Mono only for compact codes or diagnostics.

## Composition

- Make destination choices large enough to explain themselves with an icon,
  title, and one short description.
- Group related information in paper-like cards; let borders do most of the
  grouping and save stronger shadows for major panels.
- Use 44px as the minimum interactive target. Common interior gaps are 12–24px;
  major panel spacing is 24–36px.
- Pair selection, readiness, safety, and error colors with text or icon labels.

## Content and accessibility

- Speak warmly and directly. Name the next action with a short verb.
- State offline, preview-only, safety, and progression boundaries where the
  player makes the decision.
- Do not use manipulative urgency, reward pressure, or ambiguous connectivity
  claims.
- Honor reduced motion, larger text, and high-contrast paths without changing
  the underlying information or control order.

## Dark mode

The current DayKare custom panels are light-first. The design system’s dark
theme was deliberately derived from the cocoa and paper palette while
preserving the source activity hues and foreground contrast. Do not claim it is
a direct screenshot match to an existing DayKare dark interface.
